# FDD Operator CD inlay: English translation

This is a translation of `FDD_Operator_CD_Inlay.jpg`, the back inlay of the SEGA Saturn FDD Operator CD (HSS-0128). The reading aids printed above the kanji are left out.

## Spines

- Left: SEGA SATURN · **SEGASATURN FDD OPERATOR** · HSS-0128
- Right: SEGA SATURN · **Sega Saturn FDD Operator** · HSS-0128

## ⚠ Caution: notes on using the Sega Saturn FDD Operator

**● Do not scratch or dirty the disc ●**
Handle the disc carefully so that neither side gets scratched or dirty. Do not bend the disc or enlarge its centre hole.

**● Do not write on the disc ●**
Do not write on either side of the disc or put stickers on it.

**● When wiping off dirt ●**
Use a soft cloth, such as one for cleaning lenses, and wipe gently in straight lines from the centre out to the edge. Do not use thinner, benzine or similar solvents.

**● Take care where you store it ●**
After use, put the disc back in its original case. Store it away from hot or humid places.

> The Sega Saturn FDD Operator is software made only for the Sega Saturn FDD. Never play it in an ordinary CD player or similar device, because doing so could damage headphones, speakers and other equipment.
>

> Do not connect the Sega Saturn to a projection TV (a TV that projects onto a screen). Afterimages can cause screen burn-in.

## Notes

- **Product number:** the inlay gives **HSS-0128**, which is the floppy drive's model number, and the catalogue number 670-8891. The disc's own header gives 610-6276. The CD was evidently sold or bundled with the drive.
- **Warning about the CD player:** track 1 is computer data. Played as audio in an ordinary CD player, it comes out as loud noise that could damage headphones or speakers. Track 2 is the spoken audio warning.
