Build of GAME_BASIC_for_SEGA_SATURN_Tutorial_Manual_EN.pdf

From this folder:

  python3 build.py ../GAME_BASIC_for_SEGA_SATURN_Tutorial_Manual_EN.md ../GAME_BASIC_for_SEGA_SATURN_Tutorial_Manual_EN.pdf

Needs pandoc, node with playwright (Chromium), and python3 with beautifulsoup4 and pypdf.

- Same look as the Reference Manual (style.css is shared in content with reference_manual_pdf_build).
- Every chapter starts on a new page. Sections (Introduction, Contents, 2.1, 2.2 ...) flow on, but a section
  heading that would land near the bottom of a page (less than about eight lines of room) moves to the next page.
- Smaller headings are kept on the same page as at least five lines of what follows them.
- Page numbers count every page, covers included, so the footer matches a PDF viewer's page number.
- The covers are copied from the first and last page of the existing PDF (or a 2-page PDF given with
  --covers covers.pdf) and scaled to fill A4. A blank page follows the front cover, and one or two
  blank pages precede the back cover, so the page count is always even and the back cover prints on the back of the last sheet.
